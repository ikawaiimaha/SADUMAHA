# SADU research and claims brief

Use this brief when researching, writing or presenting SADU. The subject is this repository's Arabic-first exhibition-workflow prototype for proposed use by the Sharjah Department of Culture. Its immediate value proposition is making unresolved handoffs visible: who must act, what is missing and which evidence permits the next step. The current collection demonstration remains the centre of the pitch.

## Research scope

Research collection, packing, approvals, publication, evidence handling and recovery against the existing implementation and institutional requirements. Al-Sadu weaving provides naming and design inspiration. Sadu Capital is a separate investment firm and is outside this scope unless investor research is explicitly requested. A shared name establishes no affiliation.

Use this starting instruction for future research:

> SADU refers exclusively to our Arabic-first exhibition-workflow prototype for proposed use by the Sharjah Department of Culture. Evaluate its collection, approvals, publication and evidence workflows. Treat Al-Sadu weaving only as naming inspiration; exclude Sadu Capital unless explicitly researching investors. Separate verified external facts, demonstrated capabilities, design interpretations and proposed work. Cite the exact source and state what it does and does not establish.

## Evidence and claim boundaries

| Claim type | Evidence required | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| External fact | Primary source, relevant passage, event date and publication date where available | A source describing an institution or craft does not endorse this product. |
| Implemented capability | Relevant code, test result and scope of the browser or service check | A local test does not establish deployment, authenticated institutional authority or measured staff savings. |
| Institutional requirement | Attributed instruction, applicable scope and confirmed decision authority | A message, transcript or secondary summary remains evidence for review until the authorized instruction is confirmed. |
| Design interpretation | Explicitly labelled product choice and its purpose | A metaphor or design preference does not become an external mandate. |
| Proposed capability | Named dependency, acceptance criteria and unresolved questions | Describe it as proposed until implemented and verified in the stated environment. |

Keep original source references and revision history. Do not infer a missing acknowledgment, convert a recorded instruction into another person's approval, or silently correct conflicting dates. Restrict private source material to its permitted audience; use synthetic records for the presentation.

## UNESCO reference

[UNESCO Decision 20.COM 7.C.1](https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/20.COM/7.C.1) belongs to the 2025 committee session. It transfers Al Sadu, traditional weaving skills in the United Arab Emirates, to the Representative List and selects the safeguarding programme as a good safeguarding practice. G.1 discusses documentation and knowledge transfer; G.3 discusses the participating actors. The decision does not mandate this software or specify its database enums, interface or visual identity.

The heritage export uses a versioned **local interpretation** of those topics, not UNESCO certification. Transmission and safeguarding measures reference G.1; social actors reference G.3. Material provenance remains a local descriptive field without a direct criterion mapping in this decision. Earlier exports may contain the previous mapping; the correction does not rewrite saved evidence or issue a compliance determination. The vocabulary values themselves remain unchanged.

“Structure, not motif” is a SADU design principle. Keep the existing visual identity without describing decorative choices as UNESCO requirements. A suitable optional naming explanation is:

> Inspired by the connections within a woven structure, SADU brings exhibition responsibilities, evidence and decisions into a shared record.

> يستلهم سدو ترابط خيوط النسيج ليجمع مسؤوليات المعرض وأدلته وقراراته في سجل مشترك.

This describes the product intent; the current demonstrations remain separate synthetic records.

## What the current pitch can demonstrate

The guided collection example demonstrates accepted backup responsibility, pickup-date validation and packing prerequisites. The separate detailed collection example exposes technical clearance, Finance cost review and completion evidence. Collection readiness does not mean transport is booked or the artwork collected. The print example demonstrates revision-specific review, supplier acknowledgment and a correction hold before replacement. None sends a live message, releases money or controls a physical press.

The page explains the wider exhibition journey as context. It does not prove a unified institutional backend. The separate local collection service shares readiness rules with the presentation and persists records within a single process; browser checkpoints and simulated role selectors are not institutional access controls.
