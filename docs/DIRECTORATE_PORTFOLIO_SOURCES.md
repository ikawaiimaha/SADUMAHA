Reviewed 18 September 2026. Applies only to the separate fictional SADU mockup.

## Adopted design

The owner clarified that the Director oversees whether departments under his direction deliver activities on schedule. Routine execution and handovers remain with managers and assigned teams. The new view groups programme references into awards and criticism; forums, festivals and arts; and publishing and institutional output. These are proposed navigation groups, not an approved organisation chart.

Eleven entries are shown: ten programme/output references and one fictional exhibition case. Only the fictional case has a connected delivery forecast. Unknown reports are explicitly missing, never green. Filtering an area does not change the scope of the headline totals. The reference entries are not eleven confirmed active projects, and the totals must not be described as institutional performance figures.

The live page does not assign award judging, publication authorization, custody, expenditure, or personal operational duties to the Director. His documented Secretary-General roles are programme-specific context; they do not imply those permissions in SADU.
